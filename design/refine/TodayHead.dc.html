Refinement 3
Today should open the way every other screen now does
On rail screens each area opens with the big serif day sentence — except Today, which keeps its older header anatomy (a plain title, the ops line, then the strip). Today IS the day; it should wear the day’s own head, not predate it. One anatomy everywhere, and the sentence finally lives on the screen it describes.
